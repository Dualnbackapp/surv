Offline survival manual

Surv

The survival manual that keeps working when everything else stops. 59 written guides, 27 scenarios and 14 tools, compiled into the app — no account, no server, no download.

Turn on airplane mode and use the whole app. Nothing changes. That is the entire point.

What is inside

  • 59 guides — stopping a bleed, CPR, choking, anaphylaxis, drowning, burns, hypothermia, finding and purifying water, food storage, fire, shelter, navigation, signalling, security, sanitation, power, knots.
  • 27 scenarios, each in before / during / after — earthquake, blackout, flood, wildfire, pandemic, economic crisis, long-term collapse, no internet, cyberattack, civil unrest, armed conflict, avalanche, house fire, lost outdoors.
  • 14 tools — a solar and celestial compass that computes true north from the sun's real position, your coordinates to read out to rescuers, waypoints and a trail map that needs no tiles, Morse SOS on the torch, a 3 kHz whistle, a CPR metronome, bleach dosing and boiling time by altitude, a nearby link that messages other phones with no network at all.
  • A local decision engine. Type what is actually happening and get the gesture to do first, with the guide it came from.

Free forever

Every life-saving guide, the Impact mode, the guided triage, the compass, your coordinates, the SOS light, the whistle and the CPR metronome are free, with no account and no subscription. We do not charge someone who is bleeding.
