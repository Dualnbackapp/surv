Effective 2 September 2026 · Surv (iOS)

Surv is a reference manual, not a rescue service. It gives no diagnosis, names no medicine and gives no dose. In an emergency, call your emergency number first — 112 in Europe, 911 in the US and Canada, 999 in the UK.

What Surv is

Surv is an offline survival manual: written guides, scenarios, checklists and tools that work with no network. Its content is general information, written and reviewed with care, drawing on published guidance such as the European Resuscitation Council recommendations and public civil-protection material. It is not personalised advice.

What Surv is not

  • Not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or monitor anything, and it does not replace a doctor, emergency services or a first aid course.
  • Not an artificial intelligence. The local engine is a set of hand-written triage rules over the guides in the app. There is no language model on board and none is called over the network.
  • Not a street map. The trail map draws your own points and your own track. Carry a paper map.
  • Not a substitute for a radio or a rescue beacon. The nearby link reaches a few dozen metres, phone to phone.

Your responsibility

You decide what to do. Survival situations are unpredictable and depend on facts the app cannot know. Always follow the instructions of the emergency services and local authorities where they differ from anything you read here. Check that any practice described is lawful where you are — trapping, hunting, radio transmission and self-defence are regulated almost everywhere, including in a crisis.
